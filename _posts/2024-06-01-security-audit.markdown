# Conducted a controls and compliance assessment and provided recommendations to company stakeholders to mitigate risks and avoid fines based on best practices for NIST CSF, PCI DSS, GDPR, SOC 1 & SOC 2.
description: Completed a controls and compliance checklist to identify safeguards, document gaps, and recommend practical improvements.
scenario: A fictional business expanding its online operations needed an internal review of its security controls, operational readiness, and compliance exposure.
objective: Identify control gaps that could increase the risk of a data breach, operational disruption, or noncompliance.
tools:
  - NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
  - Controls and compliance checklist
  - Risk assessment and asset review
tasks:
  - Reviewed the audit scope, goals, critical assets, and risk considerations.
  - Evaluated existing administrative and technical controls against the checklist.
  - Documented control gaps and translated them into practical recommendations.
findings:
  - title: Access control
    detail: Access to sensitive customer information was broader than necessary, increasing exposure if an account were compromised.
  - title: Recovery readiness
    detail: Disaster-recovery planning needed to be formalized so the organization could restore operations after a disruptive event.
  - title: Authentication policy
    detail: Password requirements and enforcement needed stronger, documented controls to reduce account-security risk.
recommendations:
  - Apply least-privilege access so sensitive data is available only to people who need it for their role.
  - Establish and test a disaster-recovery plan with defined ownership and recovery priorities.
  - Formalize password requirements and reinforce them through technical controls and workforce awareness.
